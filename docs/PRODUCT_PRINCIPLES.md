# Product Principles

A feature is eligible for BASOUL OS only when it solves a real workflow problem and satisfies at least one value condition: save time, reduce errors or improve decision making.

## Product gates
- Does it reduce steps or cognitive load?
- Does it belong to a clear workspace?
- Is its business logic reusable outside React components?
- Is it responsive, touch-friendly, keyboard-friendly and accessible?
- Can its outcome be measured or tested?
- Are the affected docs, changelog and test checklist updated?

## Experience rules
- The dashboard answers: **What should I do now?**
- The Decision Layer consumes normalized business state, not raw UI state.
- Creation must be consistent across modules.
- Mobile should express the user’s role, not imitate the desktop interface.
