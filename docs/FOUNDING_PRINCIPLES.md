# Founding Principles

1. Decision before data.
2. AI is a core layer, not a decorative feature.
3. No business logic in the UI.
4. Everything is modular and replaceable.
5. Performance is a feature.
6. Security and tenant isolation are designed in.
7. Every feature solves a real operational or engineering problem.
8. Every stable release is production-minded and testable.
9. Quality outranks speed; delivery outranks perfectionism.
10. Every recommendation must be explainable.
11. Every engineering decision should be explainable, measurable, and improvable.
12. AI assists the licensed professional; it does not impersonate an approving authority.
