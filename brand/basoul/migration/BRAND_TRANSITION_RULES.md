# Brand Transition Rules

## Identity architecture

- **YOSSEUF RADWAN** = Founder / Personal Brand.
- **BASOUL** = Technology Masterbrand.
- **BASOUL AI** = artificial-intelligence products and gateway experiences.
- **BASOUL OS** = operating-system/workspace product family.
- **BASOUL ONE** = unified intelligent interface and companion family, subject to R1 integration approval.
- **BASOUL LABS** = research and experimental technology family, subject to R2 governance approval.

## Future product naming pattern

Use `BASOUL + functional family name` in uppercase masterbrand form: BASOUL AI, BASOUL OS, BASOUL ONE, BASOUL LABS. New names require product-architecture fit, legal clearance, domain/handle review, localization review, and visual-lockup approval.

## Protection rules

- Never convert founder biography, authorship, copyright, personal social profiles, or personal legal identity into BASOUL.
- Never edit approved BASOUL artwork to fit an implementation. Implementation must fit approved artwork.
- Never rewrite historical YOSSEUF releases or Git history.
- During transition, state the relationship plainly: "BASOUL, founded by YOSSEUF RADWAN," where approved.
- Compatibility labels must be time-bound, measurable, and reversible.
