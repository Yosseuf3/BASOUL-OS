# Final Brand Decision

## Status

**PRIMARY FINAL CANDIDATE: BASOUL**  
**BACKUP CANDIDATE: BASTOUL**

This document records the human-review outcome of Brand Genesis v1. It is a naming decision record, not a legal trademark-clearance opinion and not authorization to rename production systems.

## Why BASOUL advanced

BASOUL remained one of the strongest human-preferred candidates after the deterministic Brand Genesis scoring, product-family stress test, and subsequent qualitative review. It is short, pronounceable, visually compact, and works cleanly across the intended product architecture:

- BASOUL
- BASOUL AI
- BASOUL OS
- BASOUL Cloud
- BASOUL Studio
- BASOUL Labs
- BASOUL Platform
- BASOUL One

The private brand story can preserve the family-derived origin discussed during naming exploration while allowing BASOUL to stand independently as a global masterbrand.

## Backup

BASTOUL remains the designated fallback candidate. It preserves the original personal derivation more explicitly, but is longer and less compact as a masterbrand.

## Required gates before any rebrand

Before BASOUL is promoted from final candidate to production brand, complete and document:

1. Counsel-led trademark clearance in priority jurisdictions, especially Classes 9 and 42.
2. Direct registrar/RDAP confirmation of priority domains and acquisition plan.
3. Handle availability/acquisition plan for priority social and developer platforms.
4. Native-speaker and cultural screening in priority markets.
5. Corporate-name/confusion review.
6. Written GO / CONDITIONAL GO / NO-GO decision.

## Freeze rule

Until the above gates are complete, do **not** rename repositories, packages, applications, deployments, databases, environment variables, production assets, or YVL artifacts.

## Decision record

- Backup candidate: **BASTOUL**
- Brand Genesis engine result remains preserved as research evidence.
- Production naming remains unchanged.
